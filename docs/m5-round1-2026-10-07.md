## Why this slice

The civ-lab docs do not define M5. The only definition is Astra review 3,
finding 10 (`universe-atlas/docs/handoff-2026-10-06/astra-problems/answer3.md`),
which lists five deliverables:

1. a productive-capacity stock;
2. replenishable colony reserves;
3. failure-reason-aware targeting;
4. recovery that changes collapse drivers;
5. an E0-start panel with controlled interventions.

**Round 1 is deliverable 3 only, and it is opt-in.** This was decided by Opus
5.5 on Kasper's instruction; Kasper can overrule it. Deliverable 3 was chosen
for three reasons:

- It is the most self-contained: target memory in `planet.mjs` plus route
  filtering and launch in `m2b.mjs`.
- It can be tested at fixture level.
- Review 3 names its diagnostic gap explicitly: 82% of lonely-earthlike
  launches went to previously failed targets.

The opt-in gate is what reconciles M5 with the rule that existing digests must
stay byte-identical. Default runs are unchanged by construction.

## What changed

The policy is `targetingPolicy: 'm5-targeting-1'`. It is accepted by
`createDomainDefinition`, `createDomain` and `createScenario`. An unknown value
throws `RangeError`. Planet-only paths (`createDefinition`/`createPlanet`) also
reject it. There is no CLI flag, scenario-file field or override key yet.

When the policy is on:

- **Failure payloads** gain `reason`, `year` (the event's `dateYear`) and
  `transportTier`. The reason is one of four values:

  | Failure | Reason |
  |---|---|
  | `VOYAGE_LOST` | `transit-loss` |
  | Arrival rejected because another lineage occupies the destination | `occupied` |
  | Arrival rejected because the destination is uninhabitable | `uninhabitable` |
  | `COLONY_LOST` or a colony `EXTINCTION` | `colony-died` |

- **Each reason has its own retry delay**, counted from the year of the failure
  event. All values are [scenario]:

  | Reason | Delay | Early retry allowed |
  |---|---|---|
  | `transit-loss` | 500 years | Yes, if transport tier `tiers[3]` is now strictly higher than the failed voyage's tier |
  | `occupied` | 2,000 years (`OCCUPANCY_YEARS`) | No |
  | `uninhabitable` | 10,000 years, a resurvey | No |
  | `colony-died` | 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000 / 8,000 years, from an integer table, capped | No |

- **Each memory entry** is `{body, count, eventId, failureYear, reason,
  transportTier, untilYear}`. Failure handling no longer clears occupancy
  signals, so the two memories are independent.
- **Success evidence:** founding and reinforcement SIGNALs carry `eventYear`.
  - A delivered own-lineage success that is strictly newer than a remembered
    failure clears that failure.
  - A failure older than a known success is ignored.
  - The greatest success `eventYear` survives later deliveries of older or
    generic signals.
- **LAUNCH events** record `priorFailure` (a reason or `none`) and
  `priorFailureActive` (`0` or `1`).
- **Constants** sit in a frozen `TARGETING_M5` object in `src/m2b.mjs`. The
  habitat weights stay at 1000:200.
- **Manifest:**
  - `scenarioPriors.targetingPolicy` is added only when the policy is on.
  - `def.rngManifestDigest` is the digest of the manifest without that block,
    and also exists only when the policy is on.
  - Every RNG address site uses `def.rngManifestDigest ?? def.manifestDigest`:
    `civ-input.address`, `hazard.threshold`, narrative names,
    refine-cell `rng` and the placement cache, and toy `magnitudeAddress`.
  - So opted-in runs draw the same variates as default runs, which makes
    before/after comparisons controlled. Recipe and result digests still
    differ.

Files changed: `src/{civ-input,domain,hazard,m2b,narrative,planet,refine-cell,refinement-contract,scenario,toy}.mjs`,
`tests/civ-helpers.mjs` (`runForced` takes optional options),
`tests/m5-targeting.test.mjs` (new, 8 tests),
`docs/transition-table-v1.md` (M5 round 1 section).

**Runner finding, fixed in the second Astra run.** In the first version,
`rngManifestDigest` was set on every definition. That silently disarmed two
negative controls:

- `horizon-prefix`: hashing the horizon into the manifest;
- `m4b-horizon-prefix`: the old `forced.ticks` bundle.

Both tests override `manifestDigest` and expect the RNG draws to change. The
field now exists only when the policy is on. Both controls pass unedited.

## Not done, and next rounds

- No perf, perf:panel, ensemble or plausibility runs. So there is no
  measurement yet of how the policy changes repeated failed-target launches.
- That measurement needs an ensemble in a quiet window, plus CLI and
  ensemble-tool support for the policy. Both are round 2 candidates.
- Risk: the 10 kyr `uninhabitable` resurvey may starve doomed-star
  colonisation. This is unmeasured.
- Out of scope for this round:
  - deliverables 1, 2, 4 and 5;
  - demand- and capacity-aware target weights;
  - arrival deaths and reinforcement allocation (review 3 finding 5);
  - making the policy the default;
  - the Node 22 vs 24 digest check;
  - a delivered-knowledge diagnostic for default runs (the M4 count stays
    omniscient).
